ABOUT // ME

Hi, I'm Sage. I design products people understand on the first try, combining a graphic designer's eye with a researcher's curiosity.

I'm a UX/UI master's student at New York Institute of Technology. My process starts with users: interviews, journey maps and usability tests guide every decision, and I iterate in Figma until the experience feels effortless. Building games in Unity has taught me a lot about feedback, onboarding and keeping people engaged, and I bring that into every interface I design.

Design
User research & interviews, usability testing, personas & journey mapping, information architecture, user flows, wireframing, interactive prototyping, design systems, accessibility (WCAG 2.2), interaction & visual design
Tools
Figma (components, Auto Layout, variables, prototyping), FigJam, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign & After Effects, Maze, Miro, Unity, Blender
Code
HTML/CSS, C# (Unity), JavaScript (basic). Comfortable handing designs off to developers
